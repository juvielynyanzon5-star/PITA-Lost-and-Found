Helping Students Find Their Lost Items

Welcome to the PITA Lost and Found website. This website is created to help students and staff of PITA report and locate lost or found items inside the school.

If you have lost an item such as an ID, wallet, school supplies, or other personal belongings, you can check this website for possible found items.

Together, we can help return lost items to their rightful owners.
